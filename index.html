About Us

What is Idea AI?

Idea AI is an indie software lab founded by Ashwin Golani, focused on shipping practical, well-crafted digital products.

We started with a simple belief: most business tools are either too complex or too generic. So we build our own — lean, purposeful, and built around how people actually work.

Every product we ship is standalone, production-ready, and designed to solve one problem really well. No subscriptions to 10 different services. No steep learning curves.
